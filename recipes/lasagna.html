Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of tender pasta sheets, rich tomato sauce, flavorful meat, creamy white sauce, and melted cheese. Each layer comes together to create a delicious combination of savory, cheesy, and comforting flavors. The top develops a beautiful golden crust while the inside remains soft, creamy, and packed with flavor. This oven-baked dish is perfect for family dinners, special occasions, or whenever you crave a hearty homemade meal. Serve it warm and enjoy every delicious layer of this Italian favorite!

Ingredients

  1. For the Meat Sauce

    • 500 g ground beef
    • 2 tablespoons olive oil
    • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
    • 3 garlic cloves, minced
    • 400 g canned chopped tomatoes
    • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
    • 1 cup tomato passata or tomato puree
    • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
    • 1 teaspoon dried basil
    • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
    • Salt to taste
    • 1/2 cup water or beef stock, as needed

  2. For the White Sauce (Bechamel)

    • 4 tablespoons butter
    • 4 tablespoons all-purpose flour
    • 3 cups milk
    • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg (optional)
    • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
    • Salt to taste

  3. For the Lasagna Layers

    • 9-12 lasagna sheets, depending on the baking dish
    • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
    • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
    • 1 teaspoon dried oregano for seasoning
    • Fresh basil leaves for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Ingredients

    Finely chop the onion and mince the garlic. Measure out the remaining ingredients and keep them ready before you begin cooking. This makes the preparation process easier and more organized.

  2. Prepare the Meat Sauce

    Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook for 3-4 minutes until softened. Add the minced garlic and cook for another 30 seconds.

    Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it apart with a spoon as it cooks. Stir in the tomato paste, chopped tomatoes, tomato passata, oregano, basil, black pepper, and salt.

    Add a little water or beef stock if the sauce is too thick. Reduce the heat and let the sauce simmer for 20-30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until rich and flavorful.

  3. Prepare the White Sauce

    Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the all-purpose flour and whisk continuously for about 1-2 minutes to form a smooth paste.

    Gradually pour in the milk while whisking to prevent lumps from forming. Continue cooking and stirring until the sauce becomes smooth and thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.

    Season with salt, black pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg if desired. Remove from the heat and set aside.

  4. Prepare the Lasagna Sheets

    Check the instructions on the lasagna package. If the sheets require pre-cooking, boil them in salted water according to the package directions. Drain carefully and lay them flat to prevent sticking.

    If you are using oven-ready lasagna sheets, you can generally use them without boiling. Make sure your sauces provide enough moisture for the sheets to cook properly.

  5. Preheat the Oven

    Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Lightly grease a baking dish with butter or oil to help prevent the lasagna from sticking.

  6. Assemble the First Layer

    Spread a thin layer of meat sauce across the bottom of the baking dish. Arrange lasagna sheets over the sauce, making sure they cover most of the surface without overlapping too much.

  7. Add the Remaining Layers

    Spread some meat sauce over the pasta sheets, followed by a layer of white sauce and a sprinkle of mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers of pasta, meat sauce, white sauce, and cheese until the ingredients are used up.

    Finish with a final layer of pasta, white sauce, shredded mozzarella, and grated Parmesan cheese. This creates a delicious, golden cheese topping when baked.

  8. Bake the Lasagna

    Place the assembled dish in the preheated oven. Cover it loosely with aluminum foil and bake for approximately 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15-20 minutes, or until the cheese is golden, bubbling, and lightly browned.

    Cooking time may vary depending on the depth of the dish and whether you use fresh, pre-cooked, or oven-ready pasta sheets. Make sure the center is thoroughly heated.

  9. Let It Rest

    Remove the lasagna from the oven and allow it to rest for 10-15 minutes before cutting. This helps the layers settle and makes it easier to serve neat portions without the lasagna falling apart.

  10. Serve and Enjoy

    Cut the lasagna into square portions using a sharp knife and lift each piece carefully with a spatula. Garnish with fresh basil leaves if desired, and serve while warm.

Serving Suggestions

Serve lasagna warm with garlic bread, toasted baguette slices, or a fresh green salad. A simple salad with lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, and a light dressing balances the richness of the cheese and meat sauce. You can also sprinkle a little extra Parmesan cheese and fresh basil over each serving for additional flavor. For a complete Italian-style meal, pair it with roasted vegetables or steamed vegetables on the side.

Chef's Tips

  • For a flavorful sauce: Allow the meat sauce to simmer long enough for the tomatoes, herbs, and seasonings to blend together.
  • For a smooth white sauce: Add milk gradually while whisking continuously to prevent lumps.
  • For perfect layers: Spread the sauces evenly and avoid using too much filling in a single layer.
  • For a golden topping: Remove the foil during the final stage of baking so the cheese can brown and become bubbly.
  • For easier serving: Let the lasagna rest for 10-15 minutes after baking before cutting.
  • For a vegetarian version: Replace the ground beef with chopped mushrooms, spinach, zucchini, or a mixture of vegetables and lentils.
  • For make-ahead preparation: Assemble the lasagna ahead of time, refrigerate it safely, and bake when needed. If baking it directly from the refrigerator, allow extra cooking time and check that the center is hot.

Final Note

Homemade lasagna is a comforting Italian classic that combines tender pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy white sauce, and melted cheese in every bite. With a little preparation and careful layering, you can create a delicious meal that is perfect for sharing with family and friends. Serve it fresh from the oven, let it rest, and enjoy this hearty, cheesy favorite!
