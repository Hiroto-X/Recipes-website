Butter Chiken

Description

Butter Chicken is a rich and creamy Indian curry featuring tender, juicy chicken pieces simmered in a velvety tomato-based gravy. Infused with aromatic spices, melted butter, and fresh cream, every bite delivers a perfect balance of smoky, savory, and mildly spicy flavors. This delicious dish pairs perfectly with warm naan, buttery roti, or steamed basmati rice, making it a comforting and satisfying meal for any occasion.

Ingredients

  1. For the first Chicken Marinade
    • 500g boneless chicken, but into bite-sized peices.
    • ½–¾ teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder
    • ¼–⅓ teaspoon salt
    • ¾-1 tablespoon lemon juice
  2. For the Second Chicken Marinade
    • ⅓ cup Greek yogurt or thick hung curd
    • ⅛ teaspoon turmeric powder (optional)
    • ¾-1 teaspoon garam masala
    • ½ teaspoon cumin powder (optional)
    • 1 teaspoon coriander powder (optional)
    • 1 teaspoon dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi, optional)
    • ¾-1 tablespoon oil
  3. For the Butter Chicken Gravy
    • 2-3 tablespoons butter or ghee
    • 1 medium onion, sliced (optional)
    • 600 g fresh tomatoes, chopped
    • ¾ tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
    • 1-2 green chilies (optional)
    • 28 whole cashews (approximately ⅓ cup)
    • ½ cup water for blending
    • 1-2 teaspoons Kashmiri red chili powder
    • 1-1½ teaspoons garam masala
    • 1-1½ teaspoons coriander powder (optional)
    • ½ teaspoon cumin powder (optional)
    • ½–¾ teaspoon salt, or to taste
    • 1 teaspoon sugar
    • ½ tablespoon dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
    • 1½ cups hot water for the gravy
    • ⅓ cup heavy cream or whipping cream (approximately 80–100 ml)
  4. Optional Whole Spices
    • 1 cinnamon stick, approximately 2 inches long
    • 2-4 green cardamom pods
    • 2-4 cloves
  5. For Granishing
    • 2 tablespoons fresh coriander leaves, finely chopped
    • A little extra fresh cream
    • A small knob of butter

Instructions

Follow the Instruction to make your butter chicken crispy and Tasty.

  1. Prepare the chicken: Cut the boneless chicken into bite-sized pieces. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture.
  2. First marination: In a large bowl, combine the chicken with Kashmiri red chili powder, salt, and lemon juice. Mix well so every piece is coated evenly. Cover and let it rest for 20 minutes.
  3. Second marination: Add thick yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala, turmeric, cumin powder, coriander powder, kasuri methi, and oil to the chicken. Mix thoroughly. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes. For better flavor, marinate overnight.
  4. Prepare the tomato-cashew puree: If using onions, heat a little oil in a pan and sauté the sliced onions until lightly golden. Let them cool. Blend the chopped tomatoes, cashews, sautéed onions, and water until you get a smooth puree.
  5. Cook the whole spices: Heat butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves. Stir until the spices begin to sizzle and release their aroma.
  6. Add the aromatics: Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies to the pan. Cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring frequently, until the raw smell disappears. Be careful not to burn the garlic.
  7. Add the ground spices: Turn the heat to low and stir in Kashmiri red chili powder, garam masala, cumin powder, and coriander powder. Mix quickly so the spices do not burn.
  8. Cook the tomato gravy: Pour the prepared tomato-cashew puree into the pan. Mix well and bring it to a gentle boil. Reduce the heat and simmer for about 12-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the gravy thickens.
  9. Adjust the gravy: Add hot water and mix until the gravy reaches your preferred consistency. Simmer for another 10 minutes until the sauce becomes smooth and flavorful.
  10. Cook the marinated chicken: Heat a little butter or oil in a separate pan. Add the marinated chicken pieces in a single layer, leaving some space between them. Cook in batches if necessary. Turn the pieces and cook until lightly browned.
  11. Combine the chicken and gravy: Add the cooked chicken to the tomato gravy. Mix gently so the chicken is coated with the sauce. Cover and simmer for approximately 5-7 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
  12. Finish the butter chicken: Add salt and sugar to taste. Crush the kasuri methi between your palms and stir it into the gravy. Turn off the heat and gently mix in the fresh cream.
  13. Garnish and serve: Garnish with fresh coriander leaves, a little cream, and a small knob of butter. Serve hot with naan, roti, or steamed basmati rice.

Serving Suggestions

Butter Chicken tastes best when served hot with garlic naan, butter naan, roti, or steamed basmati rice. Serve it with sliced onions and a lemon wedge for extra freshness.

Chef's Tips

  • Marinate the chicken for several hours for better flavor.
  • Blend the tomato and cashew mixture until smooth.
  • Add fresh cream and kasuri methi near the end.
  • Serve immediately while the gravy is warm and creamy.

Final Note

Butter Chicken combines tender chicken, aromatic spices, and rich, buttery gravy into a delicious Indian classic. Serve it fresh with your favorite bread or rice and enjoy!
